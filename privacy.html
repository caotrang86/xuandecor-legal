Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how vanlinh, an internal XUANDECOR marketing assistant, handles information when connected to TikTok developer APIs.

Information we process

vanlinh may process TikTok authorization tokens, basic account information returned by TikTok, video files selected by authorized operators, captions, hashtags, publishing status, and API logs needed to operate and troubleshoot the integration.

How information is used

Information is used only to prepare, validate, upload, and publish marketing videos for accounts that XUANDECOR owns or is authorized to manage. It is also used to keep local logs of successful drafts, failed uploads, and technical blockers.

Data sharing

vanlinh does not sell personal data. Data is shared with TikTok only as required to use TikTok's official APIs. Data may be processed locally on XUANDECOR-controlled machines and storage.

Data retention

Drafts and logs are retained only as long as needed for content review, troubleshooting, and business record keeping. Authorization tokens can be revoked through TikTok account settings at any time.

Security

Access tokens and API credentials should be stored only in local environment files or secure secret stores. They should not be shared in public chats or committed to public repositories.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact XUANDECOR at caotrang.xuandecor@gmail.com.
